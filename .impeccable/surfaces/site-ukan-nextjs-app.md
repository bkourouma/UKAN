# Surface brief: UKAN website (all routes, FR and EN)

Scope: the whole site in `site/ukan-nextjs` (home first, then every inner page inherits). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: UK alumni in Côte d'Ivoire, mostly on a phone from a WhatsApp link; learn in seconds they are eligible, see a living network, send a membership request. Action: a WhatsApp (or email) membership request, composed on the page.

Constraints: brand charter pinned (colours, Source Serif 4 + IBM Plex Sans, logo rules, tricolour rule, one orange button per screen, blue text on orange). No photographs yet; no invented testimonials, member counts or partners; 2030 figures are targets. The user rejects: cold or corporate, and "Africa" clichés (folkloric motifs, masks, savannah).

Unresolved: real photographs, fee amount, online form link, social links.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The network is a cloth woven from strips, like Kita: each alumnus, each programme a strip sewn edge to edge. Refuses the institutional template of a blue header over alternating sections of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: UKAN blue is the cloth (committed, about 60 % of the surface), visible seams every strip on a 12-strip loom grid; white strips carry reading; orange, green and pale blue appear only as weft blocks, rectangular, square-cornered, placed in a checker offset from strip to strip. The tricolour rule runs as warp stripes and selvedges. Source Serif 4 large and warm for headlines, Plex for everything set small. No zigzags, diamonds, wax prints or fabric textures: construction, not folklore.

STORY: I studied in the UK; this is my people in Abidjan; there is a place for me; I send my request now.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Blue cloth with seams. Strips 1–7: headline "Vous avez étudié au Royaume-Uni. Votre réseau est ici." in white serif, lede below. Strips 8–12: a white woven panel, the "métier" (loom): choose your UK link, name, institution, year, then one orange button "Envoyer ma demande sur WhatsApp" that opens a prefilled message. Weft blocks in orange and green stagger behind the panel and off the right edge. Mobile: headline, then the panel, full width.

FORM: Kita strip-weave, my own first-ranked grounded candidate (IMPECCABLE'S PICK), chosen by the user over the assigned Plateau direction. seed: none (unavailable). Signature interaction: as the visitor fills the loom, the panel's selvedge strip weaves in block by block (one block per answered field), and turns green when the request is ready (green is the charter's confirmation colour, § 1; orange stays reserved for the single send button). Motion grammar: one load sequence where the hero weft blocks slide in along their strips like a shuttle; nothing else animates on its own.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
