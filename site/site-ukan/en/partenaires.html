UKAN brings together professionals and leaders trained in the United Kingdom, across every sector of the Ivorian economy.

What you gain

Why partner with UKAN

A talent pool

People trained at UK institutions, in finance, energy, law, health, engineering and digital.

Targeted visibility

Your initiatives presented to a structured professional network, at our meetings and in the quarterly newsletter.

A bridge between two countries

A concrete relay for cooperation projects between the United Kingdom and Côte d’Ivoire.

Forms of partnership

Four ways to work together

Institutional partnership

Framework agreement with a public institution, an embassy or a university.

Event sponsorship

Association of your brand with the Annual Alumni Day or a training session.

Philanthropy

Financial or in-kind support for a programme, notably UKAN Impact.

Services

Network experts working on a specific project, under contract.
