Am I eligible?

Three situations make you eligible. If one of them describes you, you can join UKAN.

You studied in the United Kingdom

At a UK higher education institution, at any level and in any year.

You trained there

Professional training, continuing development, an exchange or a research programme in the UK.

You held a UK scholarship

Chevening or any other academic programme funded by a UK institution.

The direction

The 2026–2030 Strategic Plan

Adopted to turn the findings of the revitalisation committee into a multi-year programming framework, with owners, deadlines, resources and indicators.

It rests on six pillars: member mobilisation, governance, professional value creation, partnerships, resources, and communications.

Read the plan
  • Pillar 1 — Members, mobilisation and community
  • Pillar 2 — Governance and professionalisation
  • Pillar 3 — Employability, leadership and entrepreneurship
  • Pillar 4 — Partnerships and cooperation
  • Pillar 5 — Resources and sustainability
  • Pillar 6 — Communications, visibility and influence
