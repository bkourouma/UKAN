Privacy policy

What UKAN collects, why, and what you can request.

What this site collects

This site has no form, no user account and no advertising tracker. It sets no analytics cookies. Browsing these pages leaves no personal data with UKAN.

What UKAN collects elsewhere

When you write to UKAN by WhatsApp or email, or when you complete a membership form, the association collects the information you provide: identity, contact details, UK academic background, professional situation and areas of interest.

Why

This information is used to process membership applications, maintain the member database, keep you informed about the network’s activities and connect you with other members. It is never sold, rented or passed to third parties for commercial purposes.

Your rights

You may at any time request access to, correction of, or deletion of the information concerning you, by writing to inzas@aol.com.
