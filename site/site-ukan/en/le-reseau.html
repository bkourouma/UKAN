About UKAN

UKAN brings together people who have studied, trained professionally or taken part in an academic programme in the United Kingdom, and who are based in Côte d’Ivoire.

UKAN gives UK alumni in Côte d’Ivoire a place to meet, share knowledge and experience, build professional relationships and contribute to the country’s development.

The association draws its strength from the diversity of its members: different sectors, generations and paths, united by a shared experience of UK education.

Our 2030 vision

To become the leading network for alumni of UK institutions in Côte d’Ivoire, recognised for the quality of its network, its leadership and its contribution to Côte d’Ivoire – UK cooperation.

Our ambition

An active network of around 150 members by 2030, with continuous improvement in the quality of member services.

Our mission

What we do

  • Promote professional networking
  • Facilitate knowledge and experience sharing
  • Develop leadership and professional excellence
  • Encourage entrepreneurship and innovation
  • Contribute to Côte d’Ivoire’s development
  • Strengthen UK – Côte d’Ivoire relations

The 2026–2030 Plan

Six pillars, five years

2026: restart. 2027: consolidation. 2028: expansion. 2029: influence. 2030: impact and preparation of the next plan.

Pillar 1 — Members

Census, database, membership drives, skills directory, thematic groups.

Pillar 2 — Governance

Statutes and internal rules, procedures manual, commissions, reporting, quarterly review.

Pillar 3 — Professional value

Mentoring, Leadership Academy, Business Network, Opportunities, training.

Pillar 4 — Partnerships

Mapping, agreements, cooperation with UK institutions, joint events.

Pillar 5 — Resources

Membership fees, sponsorship, philanthropy, calls for projects, financial control.

Pillar 6 — Communications

Identity, website, social media, newsletter, annual impact report.

Accountability

UKAN publishes a quarterly dashboard and an annual impact report. Indicators tracked: active members, renewal rate, participation in activities, mentors and mentees, opportunities shared, active partnerships, resources raised, budget execution rate, member satisfaction.
